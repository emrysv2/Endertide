ENDERTIDE Support

Common questions

Where do I download ENDERTIDE?

Open the Download page from the menu at the top. If it says the download isn't ready yet, check back soon.

Where can I find vehicle news, radar and weather?

Each one has its own page in the menu at the top: Vehicle News, Radar and Weather.

A page or image won't load. What can I try?

Refresh the page first. If it still doesn't load, try a different browser or clear your browser's cache. If nothing helps, let us know which page it was.

I found a bug or something looks wrong.

Please contact us and tell us what you were doing, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Screenshots help too.

Still need help?

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and tell us what's going on.
